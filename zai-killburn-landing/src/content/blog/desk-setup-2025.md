Every year I rebuild the desk and every year I promise it's the last time. This one might actually stick — everything earned its place over months of daily use, and every cable has a label.

### The Non-Negotiables

A monitor arm (necks are not adjustable), a single USB-C cable to the laptop, and light from the side, never from behind the screen. Everything else is negotiable.

### What I'd Skip Next Time

RGB anything, more than one mechanical keyboard on the desk at once, and the drawer of "might need it" cables. The drawer always wins. Throw it out.
