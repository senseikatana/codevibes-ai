01 — The practice

Four ways in.

No levels, no ladders, no leaderboard. Four doors into the same quiet room — choose the one your body is asking for today. Rest your cursor on each to peek inside.

01 Vinyasabreath-led flow Movement stitched to breath — one shape, one exhale — until the sequence remembers itself and you can finally stop thinking. 60 min · gently warmed · all bodies effort 02 Yinlong, held stillness Floor shapes held for three to five slow minutes. Time moves through the muscle and into the tissue underneath it. 75 min · cool room · all bodies effort 03 Restorativesupported rest Bolsters, blankets, and nothing to achieve. The poses do nothing at all — that is entirely the point. 60 min · candlelit · all bodies effort 04 Breathworkthe oldest practice Seated, guided pranayama. Breath moved in patterns until the nervous system remembers its resting rate. 45 min · still room · all bodies effort
