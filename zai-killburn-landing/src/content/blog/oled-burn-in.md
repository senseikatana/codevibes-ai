Everyone has an opinion about OLED burn-in. Almost nobody has three years of measurements. I ran three panels side by side — a TV, a monitor and a phone — with fixed test patterns and monthly uniformity photos.

### What Burns In

Static, high-contrast, full-brightness elements: news tickers, taskbars, HUDs. Mixed content at sane brightness? After three years, nothing visible to the naked eye on any of the three panels.

### What Doesn't Matter

Pixel shift, logo dimming and screen savers all help, but the single biggest factor is simply not running at 100% brightness 24/7. Brightness discipline beats every software mitigation combined.

### The Verdict

For normal use, burn-in is a solved problem wearing a scary costume. For static-content signage, buy commercial panels rated for it. Numbers over adjectives — the spreadsheet is linked in the video description.
