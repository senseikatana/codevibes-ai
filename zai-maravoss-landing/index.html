Havn — identity system — 2023
N°01Identity / logo systemHavn Kulturkai2023

A mark that takes the tide seriously.

Havn is eleven hectares of working harbour turned cultural quarter, and for thirty years it answered the city with seagull clip-art. The new mark is drawn with a compass and defended with coordinates: one circle for the basin, one wave for the waterline, one disc for the low northern sun. The construction sheet above is not decoration — it is the actual drawing the sign painters work from.

The system flexes across nineteen lockups, from a five-metre gate to a twelve-millimetre stamp, without redrawing a single curve. A logo should behave like tide: same water, different level. Eighteen months in, the harbour master still calls it “the stamp.” We have decided to take that as praise.

“An identity is not a sticker. It is a set of promises about behaviour — kept at every scale, from flag to stamp.”

Brenna — packaging system — 2024
N°02PackagingBrenna Kaffebrenneri2024

Most packaging shouts. This one makes eye contact.

A shelf of coffee is a shouting match in browns and serif promises about somebody’s grandmother’s farm. Brenna asked for loud; we built quiet with a spine. One grotesque, two weights, no photography of mountains — the label is typeset like a ticket: origin, roast, weight, date. Done.

Six roasts, two tin sizes, and a refill bag that costs less than the cappuccino you will spill on it. The rosette stamp does the selling; the second colour does the rest. Sales rose thirty-one per cent, and the founder still sets the type herself, which is the real KPI.

“The shelf is a shouting match. Volume is cheap; eye contact is expensive. We bought eye contact.”

System — 6 roasts · 250/500 g tins · refill paper · shelf talkers

Oslo Jazzfestival — poster series — 2024
N°03Poster seriesOslo Jazzfestival2024

Jazz, printed at full volume.

Forty-one concerts, two inks, one duplicator with a loose registration that we stopped fighting and started conducting. Every poster in the run of four hundred is misregistered differently — the machine solos, we comp.

Where red meets blue, the press mixes a third colour for free; we named it overprint violet, the official shade of the 2 a.m. set. Type sits on top so the instruments stay behind the voice — read first at ten metres, argue with you at one.

“Misregistration is not a mistake. It is the press signing its work.”

Edition — 41 motifs × 400 · riso A1 · numbered & signed

Voss Foundry — type design — 2021–2024
N°04Type designVoss Foundry2021–24

A grotesk with bad manners — the useful kind.

Nine styles and two axes, drawn for Norwegian signage light: flat winters, low sun, long words. The ‘a’ keeps its temper at width 62 and gets opinionated at 125. Terminals are cut straight because Norway does not do decorative, and the counters are generous because wind rattles the sign at minus eighteen.

It ships variable, and the specimen is pinned to the board below — drag it, pinch it, take the PDF home. What you are reading right now is set in it. Of course it is.

“A typeface is public infrastructure. Draw it like someone has to live with it for thirty years.”

Release — 9 styles + variable · wdth 62–125 · wght 100–900 · Latin ext
